Build a complete premium responsive website from scratch for a Kuwaiti gifting startup called:

==================================================
1. THE BRAND IDEA
==================================================

L’ANAK is a Kuwaiti gifting platform built around one idea:

You do not need an occasion to give someone something.
Sometimes, the person themselves is the reason.

Core Arabic brand message:

"مو لازم يكون فيه سبب،
أحيانًا أنت السبب."

Followed by:

"لأنّك أنت."

L’ANAK is NOT:
- a flower shop
- a coffee shop
- a food delivery app
- a coupon website

It is one destination for gifting.

Users can gift:
Coffee
Flowers
Desserts
Food
Beauty
Experiences
Gift vouchers
and other curated gifts.

The platform can feature local Kuwaiti brands.

For voucher-based gifting, the sender does NOT need to choose the exact product.

Example:
If I want to gift someone coffee, I select a coffee shop available on the platform, choose a voucher amount, add a message, and send it.

The recipient can then choose what they personally want from that brand.

This philosophy should influence the entire UX:

"I choose the gesture.
You choose what you love."
