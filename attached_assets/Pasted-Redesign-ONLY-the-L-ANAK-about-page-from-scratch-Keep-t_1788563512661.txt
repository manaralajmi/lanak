"أحيانًا ممكن تكون"

"أنت السبب."

"لأنّك أنت."

==================================================

This is extremely important.

Do NOT repeat the current problem where every sentence
is huge and every section feels stacked on top of another.

Use hierarchy:

EYEBROW
small

MAIN STATEMENT
large but controlled

SUPPORTING STORY
medium/small

EMOTIONAL STATEMENT
larger

NEGATIVE SPACE
generous

Each section should have a clear purpose.

The page should visually breathe.

Use a sophisticated editorial grid rather than centering
every piece of text.

Alternate alignment naturally between sections.
